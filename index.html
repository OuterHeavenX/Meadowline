Meadowline

A calm little city builder

  • 1Lay roads first. Houses only fill up when a road runs alongside them.
  • 2Loop some rail and drop a station beside it. Trains find their own way around.
  • 3Parks, cafés and lamps lift the mood of every home nearby.
  • 4Roads and rails bridge water on their own — just draw straight across.
  • 5Use Look to ask any home how it’s feeling, and why.
