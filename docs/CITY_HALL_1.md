## Permanent product rule

**Local buildings explain local conditions. City Hall explains citywide conditions.**

House Look remains authoritative for one household's residents, Mood, Education, Recreation, Desirability and Housing evolution. School Look remains authoritative for one School. Recreation facility Look explains local Recreation service. City Hall summarizes real citywide state and does not own Housing, Education, Recreation, City Growth, Town Goals, parcels, economy or Mobility simulation.

## Architecture

Meadowline remains a static-browser, native-ES-module, Canvas 2D, isometric, mobile-first game. City Hall introduced no framework/backend/renderer/world-size/pathfinding rewrite.

Authoritative building identity remains `src/buildings/registry.js`. Citywide aggregate reads live in `src/simulation/city-summary.js`. UI queries simulation; DOM state is not authoritative.

## Registry metadata and uniqueness

Registry ID: `cityHall`

- category: `civic`
- unlock stage: Settlement
- base placement cost: 90 coins
- footprint: 1×1
- unique: one active civic center per city
- saved level field: generic `state.level`

Placement uses the normal safe-touch contract. Removal requires confirmation and does not erase City Growth, parcels, Town Goals, population, Housing, Education, Recreation or Mobility state.

## City Hall UI

The municipal view reuses Look rather than adding a permanent dashboard.

It exposes truthful citywide sections for:

- Overview / Housing tier aggregates
- Town Goals
- City Growth
- Land
- Finances from real `S.lastPay`
- Education
- Recreation during Recreation 2.0
- Mobility from production Roads & Mobility 2.0

No fake future municipal meters are allowed.

## Roads & Mobility integration

City Hall shows only real Mobility data:

- Road tiles
- connected Road components
- Rail crossings
- active representative vehicles

It does not show Traffic Health, congestion, commute satisfaction, parking demand or any other metric Meadowline does not simulate.

Mobility truth is owned by `src/simulation/mobility.js`; City Hall only reads `getCitySummary()`.

## Recreation 2.0 integration

Recreation becomes the next real service summarized by City Hall.

The Recreation section reads authoritative simulation values from `src/simulation/recreation.js` through `getCitySummary()`:

- Facilities
- Residents served / demand
- Available capacity
- Underserved residents
- Visitors now

There is deliberately **no Recreation Health percentage** or fabricated happiness score.

A local Recreation facility remains the correct place to inspect that facility's capacity, nearby demand, street access, entrance and visitors. City Hall gives whole-city truth only.

## Town Goals / City Growth / Land

City Hall continues reading the existing authoritative Town Goal, City Growth and parcel APIs.

Recreation does not add a separate task engine. Recreation Town Goals remain demand-aware uses of the existing Goal system.

The four-stage ladder remains unchanged. Growing Town remains the current final stage.

## Finances

Treasury uses `S.coins`. Breakdown uses the real last payday categories generated by economy simulation. Before first payday, the panel shows less rather than fabricating an estimate.

Recreation 2.0 does not add Park maintenance budgets, admission prices, Recreation taxes or other invented finance categories.

## Save V3

Save key remains `meadowline.v3`.

City Hall persists ordinary building existence/position + generic `state.level`. Derived summaries are not saved.

Road/Rail crossing metadata, Recreation assignments, Recreation metrics, visitor positions and citywide summaries remain derived/runtime state where appropriate.
